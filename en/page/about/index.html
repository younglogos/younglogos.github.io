About

Things about me.

This is Junqi Yang’s personal space — an outlet for what I see, hear, and think. I love technology and history: the former gives me the “illusion” of transforming nature, while the latter satisfies my appetite for stories and gossip. In this day and age, thinking seems to have become an increasingly challenging task. Under the impact and inducement of the flood of information, our attention is endlessly consumed, and over time the mind suffers as well. In the future, thinking and expression may also be handed over to AI — humans who keep practicing these two activities might just be performing performance art. But even if only as a massage for the mind, I refuse to be someone who merely drifts along in the flood of information, submitting to whatever comes. I would rather engage in effortful thinking, and I also want to record the thoughts and experiences of everyday life.

My favorite lines of poetry (one in Chinese and one in English):

Do not go gentle into that good night,

Old age should burn and rave at close of day;

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.
