关于

北京大学生科院在读博士生一枚，写博客的缘由是想为自己的所见，所闻，所思搭建一个输出口。实验之余好扮演伪码农的角色，常有练习，鲜有佳作。博客的记录也是为了让自己更好地学习，同时起一个督促的作用。鉴于专业的特色，也会总结一些生命科学领域的研究进展。如今的时代，思考似乎成了一种麻烦事，每天的可接收的信息太多，导致光在接收的过程里就耗费了过多注意力和时间。我不愿只做一个在信息洪流里亦步亦趋，逆来顺受之人，愿多读有智识之书，做耗费脑力之思辨，最后挤出的结果输出为文字。总之，很多都还在建设之中，如内容有不合时宜之处，望读者勿字字较真……

About

I am a PhD student at the School of Life Sciences, Peking University. I started this blog to build an outlet for what I see, hear, and think. Outside the lab I enjoy playing the part of an amateur coder — practicing often, producing masterpieces rarely. Keeping this blog is also a way to learn better and keep myself accountable. Given my field, I will also write summaries of research progress in the life sciences. In this day and age, thinking seems to have become a burden: we receive so much information every day that simply consuming it drains our attention and time. I refuse to be someone who merely drifts along in the flood of information. I would rather read intellectually nourishing books, engage in effortful thinking, and squeeze the results into words. In short, much of this is still under construction — if anything here seems out of date, please don’t take every word too seriously…
