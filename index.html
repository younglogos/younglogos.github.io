这里是杨钧淇的个人空间，是我自己所见、所闻、所思的输出口。我喜欢科技和历史，前者让我有可以改造自然的”幻觉“，后者满足了我吃瓜听故事的八卦欲望。如今的时代，思考似乎成了一种越来越有挑战的任务，未来或许这项任务的权利也能让渡给AI。在信息洪流的冲击、诱导下，注意力被不断消耗着，久而久之好像心理也出了问题。我不愿只做一个在信息洪流里亦步亦趋，逆来顺受之人，我愿做耗费脑力之思辨，或是只抒发自己的感想和经验。最后挤出的结果输出为文字，即便是行为艺术，也是对心理的按摩。

最喜欢的一句诗（中文和英文各有一句）：

浩浩乎如冯虚御风，而不知其所止；飘飘乎如遗世独立，羽化而登仙。

This is Junqi Yang’s personal space — an outlet for what I see, hear, and think. I love technology and history: the former gives me the “illusion” of transforming nature, while the latter satisfies my appetite for stories and gossip. In this day and age, thinking seems to have become an increasingly challenging task — perhaps one day the right to do it can even be handed over to AI. Under the impact and inducement of the flood of information, attention is endlessly consumed, and over time the mind seems to suffer as well. I refuse to be someone who merely drifts along in this flood, submitting to whatever comes. I would rather engage in effortful thinking, or simply express my own thoughts and experiences, and squeeze the results into words — even if it is performance art, it is a massage for the mind.

My favorite lines of poetry (one in Chinese and one in English):

Vast and boundless, I ride the wind like one treading the void, not knowing where I shall stop; light and free, I stand apart from the world, transcend it, and ascend like an immortal.
